# Privacy Policy for F-Port App Store

**Effective:** August 25, 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to F-Port, the cross-platform app store for Windows, Linux, and Android. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly.

## 2. Data Controller

**Data Controller within the meaning of data protection laws:**

FreetimeMaker  
F-Port App Store

## 3. What data do we collect?

### 3.1 Automatically collected data

When you use F-Port, we collect the following data:

- **Device information:** Operating system version, device type, screen resolution
- **Usage data:** Installed apps, app usage statistics
- **Technical data:** IP address, browser or app version, timezone information

### 3.2 Voluntarily provided data

- **Account information:** Username, email address (if you create an account)
- **Feedback:** Comments, ratings, and support requests

### 3.3 Data from third parties

F-Port displays apps from various sources. The data for these apps (descriptions, icons, download links) is provided by the respective app developers.

## 4. Use of data

We use your data for the following purposes:

- **App management:** Installation, updates, and management of apps
- **App improvement:** Analysis of usage data to improve functionality
- **Support:** Providing customer support
- **Security:** Protection against fraud and abuse
- **Compliance:** Fulfillment of legal requirements

## 5. Data retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated above:

- **Usage data:** Up to 12 months
- **Account data:** As long as your account is active
- **Support data:** Up to 24 months after completion of the support case

## 6. Disclosure to third parties

We do not disclose your personal data to third parties, except:

- **With your explicit consent**
- **To fulfill legal obligations**
- **To protect our rights or prevent fraud**
- **To service providers who work for us (e.g., cloud services)**

## 7. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

- **Access:** You can request information about whether and what data we store about you
- **Deletion:** You can request deletion of your data (as far as legally permissible)
- **Correction:** You can have incorrect data corrected
- **Objection:** You can object to the processing of your data
- **Data portability:** You can receive your data in a structured format

## 8. Cookies and tracking

F-Port does not use cookies for tracking purposes. We only use technical cookies for the basic functionality of the app.

## 9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

- **Encryption:** SSL/TLS encryption for all data transfers
- **Access control:** Only authorized employees have access to your data
- **Regular updates:** We keep our systems up to date

## 10. Children and teenagers

F-Port is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children without parental consent.

## 11. Changes to this privacy policy

We reserve the right to change this privacy policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated to you in the app.
